# Biomechanics and Motion Analysis

Biomechanics applies the principles of mechanics to biological systems. Motion analysis is a crucial sub-field used to capture, quantify, and study human kinematics and kinetics, often applied in clinical diagnostics, rehabilitation, and sports performance optimization.


## Motion Capture Technologies

Modern motion analysis heavily relies on optoelectronic systems. These systems use multiple synchronized infrared cameras to track the 3D trajectories of retroreflective markers placed on specific anatomical landmarks of the subject. Force plates are also integrated to measure ground reaction forces, allowing for inverse dynamics calculations to estimate joint torques and powers.
